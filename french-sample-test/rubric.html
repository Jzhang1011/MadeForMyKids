Scoring rubric reference

What Novice and Intermediate writing and speaking look like, with short French examples. Use it to coach answers and to fill in the speaking checklist.

Writing & speaking levels

LevelFCPS creditWhat it sounds likeExample (French)Why (English)
Novice Low—Single words and memorized phrases.Bonjour. Chat. Rouge.Only isolated words; no sentences yet.
Novice Mid—Lists and memorized phrases, a few very short sentences.Je m’appelle Sam. J’aime le bleu. Chien.Some memorized sentences, mostly lists.
Novice High1 creditSimple sentences of their own on very familiar topics, with some errors.J’ai un chien. Il est petit et noir. J’aime mon chien. Ma sœur a dix ans.Several simple, complete sentences on a familiar topic; ideas are not yet connected.
Intermediate Low2 creditsCreates sentences, joins some with et / mais / parce que, adds related details.Ma famille est petite. J’ai une sœur et un chien. Le samedi, nous allons au parc parce que mon chien adore courir. Mais ma sœur préfère lire.Original sentences with simple connectors and details; mostly present tense.
Intermediate Mid3 creditsStrings of connected sentences; describes experiences and tells a simple story, sometimes in past or future.L’été dernier, nous sommes allés à la plage pour l’anniversaire de mon père. D’abord, nous avons mangé un pique-nique, ensuite nous avons nagé. L’eau était froide, mais c’était super ! L’année prochaine, je vais y retourner avec mes cousins.Connected, organized sentences with time words and past/future; errors don’t block meaning.

A level is passed only when all four sections pass. These thresholds are this site’s own practice rules.

How the writing estimate works (in your browser)

  • Novice High: at least 2 sentences, 15+ words, and a conjugated verb.
  • Intermediate Low: 4+ sentences, 40+ words, 28+ different words, 2+ kinds of connectors (et, mais, parce que, aussi, alors…), 4+ verb forms, mostly correct articles.
  • Intermediate Mid: 6+ sentences, 75+ words, 45+ different words, 3+ kinds of connectors, 6+ verb forms, past or future used, few accuracy issues.
  • Missing accents (tres → très, ecole → école, francais → français) and simple agreement slips (les chat, une grand) are shown as gentle tips, never as a fail.

How the speaking estimate works

A parent checklist (full sentences, connected ideas, understandable pronunciation, on topic, varied vocabulary, past/future or a story). 2 ticks ≈ Novice Mid, 3 ≈ Novice High, 4–5 (including “connected”) ≈ Intermediate Low, all 6 ≈ Intermediate Mid. If you record, very short recordings cap the estimate, and when the browser offers French speech recognition, the number of words heard is also considered. Everything stays on your device.

Official facts used

The only official details on this site are the section format (Reading ~30 questions, 35–40 min; Writing 3 prompts, 20–25 min, up to 2,500 characters; Listening ~30 questions, 35–40 min; Speaking 3 prompts, 20–25 min), the FCPS credit tiers (Novice High = 1, Intermediate Low = 2, Intermediate Mid = 3), and the FCPS windows (Fall Nov 4–24, 2026; Spring Mar 1–18, 2027). Everything else, including the level descriptions and thresholds, is our own practice guidance.
