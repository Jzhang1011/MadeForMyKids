Spanish STAMP 4S practice test

A friendly, beginner-level practice sample modeled on the Avant STAMP 4S format that Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS) uses for world-language credit by exam. Instructions are in English; the test content is in Spanish. Great for a parent practicing with a school-age child.
