Ten original structures, ordered easy → hard. All models were built and validated with the site's own piece system — rods and connectors on an 8×8 grid.

About these challenges

Every challenge is an original MadeForMyKids creation — not copied from any Science Olympiad test. Difficulty reflects real structural complexity (turns, junctions, symmetry), not just piece count. "The Flag" is marked as a practice simulation: it mimics competition style, but official 2027 tournament setups come only from the official rules.
