Write Better Instructions ✏️

Study the structure, write instructions a partner could build from, then self-check against the rubric.

Rubric self-check

Check each box honestly. A useful instruction lets another person build the same structure — not just a similar one.

Weak vs. improved — same structure

Both describe "First Steps" (straight connector – red rod – straight connector). Only one builds it correctly.

🚫 Weak

"Put the red piece on the blue thing and add another piece beside it."

Why it fails: connectors aren't blue, "on" and "beside" don't say which opening, and the rod length is never mentioned.

✅ Improved

"Place a straight connector with its openings facing left and right. Connect a red rod (length 3) to its right opening so the rod runs left-to-right. Connect a second straight connector to the right end of the rod, openings facing left and right."

Why it works: every piece named with size, every connection traced, left/right anchored to the front edge.

Partner tip: hand your written instructions to a partner without showing them the structure. Time them building it, then compare — every difference is a lesson about a specific sentence you wrote.
