How Write It Do It works

In the real event, one partner studies a structure and writes instructions; the other partner — who never sees the original — rebuilds it from those words alone. Scores reward how closely the reconstruction matches the target. Everything here trains that exact skill: first with guided activities, then with blind reconstructions you compare and debug yourself. Official rules: soinc.org/write-it-do-it-b.
