How to use the lab

🎯

1. Pick a verb

Start with parler — it's the pattern for thousands of -er verbs. Filter by group when you're ready for trickier ones.

🔄

2. Flip the tense

Présent is right now, passé composé is already done, futur will happen. Same verb, three time machines.

🔊

3. Say it out loud

Tap the speaker on any row to hear it, then repeat it yourself. French lives in your mouth, not just your eyes!
