What's inside

Everything a family needs to run the day

One place the kids can actually read, in one place the parents can actually manage.

📅

One shared calendar

Everyone's tasks, appointments and school events in a single timeline — one column per person, colour-coded at a glance. Syncs with Google Calendar, so nothing gets double-booked.

⭐

Stars and rewards

Kids tap tasks done and earn stars. Parents set the rewards — a movie night, a treat, a trip — and kids claim them on the board when they've earned enough.

👣

Step-by-step routines

Link a task to a Step Player routine and the iPad or iPhone walks the child through it one step at a time, with photos and spoken prompts. Mornings and bedtimes run themselves.

🔄

Google Calendar sync

Connect a Google Calendar and its events appear on the board automatically — read-only, so the board never edits your calendar. Syncs every 30 minutes.

📱

One calendar, every screen

The same day on the kitchen iPad, mum's iPhone, and dad's tablet — everyone looks at the same calendar, everywhere. Pair each screen once with a 6-character code. Free covers one screen; Plus covers as many as your family needs.

🔒

Private by design

Your family's schedule, routines and photos stay yours. No ads on the kids' screen, and nothing from your family is ever shared with anyone else.

Questions

The ones families ask first

Straight answers, no fine print.

Do I need to buy a special display?

No. The board runs on any iPad, iPhone, or Android tablet you already own. Pair it once and it becomes the family hub.

Does it work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Connect a Google Calendar and its events appear on the board automatically. The board never edits your calendar — it only reads it.

Is the Step Player connection extra?

No. Linking a task to a step-by-step routine is part of the product, free for everyone. You only pay for Plus when you want more members, more tablets, photos, Meals and Notes, or reports.

Can my kids change or delete things?

No. The tablet screen is run-only: kids can complete tasks and claim rewards, but there is no admin, no settings and no editing on it. Parents change everything from their own app.
