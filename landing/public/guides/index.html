Visual guidance, at every age

One support structure, different human needs. Make information visible, break activities into manageable steps, provide instructions through multiple channels, and let people progress at their own pace — whether they are learning a routine, need the world to be more predictable, or want to keep their independence as they age.

One system, three applications

The same design — show what is happening, explain what to do, break it into steps, guide progress, and gradually reduce assistance — serves three different needs.

Children

Learning routines and building independence.

Neurodivergent children

Predictability, transitions and reduced ambiguity.

Older adults

Memory support and continued independence.
